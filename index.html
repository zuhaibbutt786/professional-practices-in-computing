About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing integrates contemporary issues from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Cloud, Open Source, Entrepreneurship, and Digital Society. Students develop competencies aligned with ACM and IEEE guidelines while learning how responsible computing affects individuals, organizations, governments, and society.

The course emphasizes ethical reasoning, critical thinking, communication, leadership, teamwork, lifelong learning, sustainability, responsible AI, privacy, intellectual property, global software development, and emerging technologies. It is technology-independent: it teaches professional thinking rather than software tools.
