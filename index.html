About This Course

Unlike traditional professional practice courses that focus primarily on software engineering ethics, this course integrates contemporary issues from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Cloud Computing, Open Source, Entrepreneurship, and Digital Society.

Students develop professional competencies required by ACM and IEEE guidelines while learning how responsible computing affects individuals, organizations, governments, and society. The course is technology-independent — it teaches professional thinking rather than software tools.

◎

Learning Outcomes

Ethical Reasoning

Apply major ethical frameworks and professional codes to real technology dilemmas.

Professional Responsibility

Understand duty of care, accountability, whistleblowing, and the ethics of speaking up.

AI & Data Governance

Navigate responsible AI, privacy, bias, and data ethics in modern systems.

Communication & Leadership

Master technical writing, presentations, teamwork, conflict resolution and leadership.

Legal & IP Awareness

Understand software licensing, open source, patents, copyright and digital rights.

Career Readiness

Build a professional identity, personal brand, and continuous learning plan for a rapidly evolving field.
