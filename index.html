About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing is designed for undergraduate students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and related fields. It goes beyond traditional software-engineering ethics to integrate contemporary issues from AI, data, cloud, open source, entrepreneurship, and digital society.

Students develop the professional competencies expected by ACM and IEEE guidelines while learning how responsible computing affects individuals, organizations, governments, and society. The course emphasizes ethical reasoning, critical thinking, communication, leadership, teamwork, lifelong learning, sustainability, responsible AI, privacy, intellectual property, global software development, and emerging technologies.

Technology-independent. It teaches professional thinking rather than software tools.

Learning Outcomes

Ethical & Responsible Practice

Apply ethical frameworks, professional codes, and responsible-AI principles to real design and deployment decisions.

Legal & Societal Awareness

Navigate privacy, IP, liability, accessibility, and the broader societal impact of computing systems.

Professional Communication

Communicate clearly in writing and presentations; collaborate effectively in diverse and remote teams.

Career & Lifelong Learning

Build a professional identity, personal brand, and continuous learning plan for a rapidly evolving field.
