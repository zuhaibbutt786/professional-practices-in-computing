Professional Practices in Computing

An industry-standard, technology-independent course that prepares CS, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI, and Cybersecurity students for the ethical, legal, social, and professional challenges of modern computing.

Start Lecture 01 Browse modules

Weekly Modules

Each module includes multi-perspective analysis, case studies, quizzes, reflections, and assignments.

01

Professional Ethics & Responsibility

ACM/IEEE codes, ethical frameworks, real incidents, decision-making under uncertainty.

 02

Privacy, Data & Surveillance

Data protection, consent, surveillance capitalism, GDPR concepts, privacy by design.

 03

AI Ethics & Responsible AI

Fairness, accountability, transparency, NIST AI RMF, UNESCO recommendations, case studies.

 04

Intellectual Property & Open Source

Copyright, patents, licenses, FOSS ethics, contribution practices, business models.

 05

Teamwork, Leadership & Communication

Remote collaboration, conflict resolution, technical writing, presentation, inclusive teams.

Course Outcomes

  • Apply professional codes of ethics to real computing dilemmas
  • Analyze privacy, security, and AI systems from ethical and legal perspectives
  • Communicate technical ideas clearly to technical and non-technical audiences
  • Lead and contribute effectively in diverse, global, and remote teams
  • Practice lifelong learning and responsible professional development
