About the Course

Unlike traditional professional-practice courses that focus mainly on software-engineering ethics, this course integrates contemporary issues from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Open Source, Entrepreneurship and Digital Society.

Ethical Reasoning

Develop the ability to analyse complex ethical dilemmas using established frameworks and professional codes.

Professional Skills

Communication, teamwork, leadership, conflict resolution and lifelong learning for modern computing roles.

Societal Impact

Understand how computing decisions affect individuals, organisations, governments and society at large.

Weekly Modules

Each module is self-contained, production-ready and designed for active learning.

01
Introduction to Professional Practices

What it means to be a computing professional, codes of ethics, and the social context of technology.
02
Ethical Frameworks & Decision Making

Utilitarianism, deontology, virtue ethics and practical ethical reasoning for technologists.
03
Privacy, Data Protection & Surveillance

Privacy concepts, data protection principles, surveillance capitalism and professional obligations.
04
Intellectual Property & Open Source

Copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, licensing models and the open-source ecosystem.
05
Responsible AI & Algorithmic Accountability

AI ethics principles, bias, transparency, accountability and governance frameworks.
06+
Coming Soon

Security ethics, safety-critical systems, teamwork, leadership, career development and more.
