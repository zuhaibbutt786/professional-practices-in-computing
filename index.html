About this course

What you will learn

Professional ethics, responsibility, computing law, privacy, AI ethics, intellectual property, open source, technical communication, teamwork, leadership, sustainability, and career development — grounded in ACM and IEEE guidelines.

How it is taught

Case-based and scenario-based learning. Real industry incidents, ethical dilemmas, decision frameworks, quizzes, reflections, and mini-projects. Technology-independent: professional thinking, not tool training.

Weekly modules

Learning outcomes

  • Apply ethical frameworks to real computing dilemmas
  • Reason about privacy, security, and responsible AI
  • Understand IP, licensing, and open-source obligations
  • Communicate professionally and work effectively in teams
  • Navigate legal and regulatory contexts relevant to computing
  • Build habits of continuous learning and professional growth

Core references

  • Baase — A Gift of Fire
  • Quinn — Ethics for the Information Age
  • Dignum — Responsible Artificial Intelligence
  • ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
  • IEEE Code of Ethics
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework
  • UNESCO / OECD AI ethics recommendations

FAQ

Is this course language- or tool-specific?

No. It focuses on professional judgment, ethics, and practice. Any programming language or stack is fine.

How is progress tracked?

Progress is stored in your browser (localStorage). Clearing site data resets it.

Can I use this for university teaching?

Yes. Content is original synthesis for educational use. Cite primary sources for formal academic work.
