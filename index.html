About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing is designed for undergraduate students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and related fields. Unlike traditional ethics courses that focus narrowly on software engineering, this course integrates contemporary issues from AI, data science, open source, entrepreneurship, sustainability, and the digital society.

The course is technology-independent. It teaches professional thinking, ethical reasoning, critical judgment, communication, leadership, and lifelong learning — competencies required by ACM and IEEE guidelines and expected by industry from 2026 onward.

Pedagogy
Active learning • Case-based • Problem-based • Scenario-based • Reflective • Industry-oriented

Learning Outcomes

⚖️

Ethical Reasoning

Apply ethical frameworks and professional codes to real computing dilemmas involving AI, data, privacy, and safety.

🛡️

Professional Responsibility

Understand accountability, duty of care, whistleblowing, and the social impact of technical decisions.

🤖

Responsible AI & Data

Evaluate bias, fairness, governance frameworks (NIST, OECD, UNESCO), and data lifecycle ethics.

📜

Legal & IP Literacy

Navigate intellectual property, open-source licensing, privacy regulations, and professional liability at a high level.

🗣️

Communication & Leadership

Write clearly, present effectively, lead teams, resolve conflict, and collaborate across cultures and time zones.

🚀

Career & Lifelong Learning

Build a professional identity, personal brand, and continuous learning plan for a rapidly evolving field.
