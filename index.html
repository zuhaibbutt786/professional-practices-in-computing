Course at a Glance

Who it’s for

Undergraduate students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and related programmes.

What you’ll build

Professional judgment: ethics frameworks, responsible AI thinking, privacy analysis, communication, teamwork, and career habits that last beyond any single tool or language.

How you’ll learn

Case studies, scenarios, decision simulators, quizzes, reflection, and industry-grounded assignments. Active learning, not passive reading.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply ethical frameworks to real computing dilemmas (ACM/IEEE aligned).
  • Analyse privacy, security, and AI risks from professional, legal, and societal angles.
  • Communicate technical ideas clearly in writing and presentation.
  • Collaborate effectively in teams, including remote and global settings.
  • Plan continuous professional development and personal branding.
