About This Course

Professional Practices in Computing is an interdisciplinary course that goes beyond traditional software-engineering ethics. It integrates contemporary issues from AI, data science, cybersecurity, open source, entrepreneurship, and digital society.

Ethical Reasoning

Frameworks, dilemmas, and decision-making for real professional situations.

Responsible AI & Data

Privacy, fairness, accountability, and governance of intelligent systems.

Communication & Leadership

Technical writing, presentations, teamwork, conflict resolution, and remote collaboration.

Law, IP & Open Source

Licensing, patents, copyright, digital rights, and professional responsibility.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply ethical frameworks to computing dilemmas across AI, data, security, and software.
  • Interpret professional codes (ACM, IEEE) and legal concepts relevant to computing practice.
  • Communicate effectively in writing and presentations; lead and collaborate in teams.
  • Evaluate privacy, security, accessibility, sustainability, and societal impact of systems.
  • Plan continuous professional development, certifications, and career strategies.
