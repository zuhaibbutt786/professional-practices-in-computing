About the Course

Unlike traditional professional practice courses that focus mainly on software engineering ethics, this course integrates contemporary issues from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud, Open Source, Entrepreneurship, and Digital Society.

Students develop competencies aligned with ACM and IEEE guidelines while learning how responsible computing affects individuals, organizations, governments, and society. The course is technology-independent: it teaches professional thinking rather than specific tools.
