About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing is an interdisciplinary course that develops the professional competencies required by ACM and IEEE guidelines. Students learn ethical reasoning, critical thinking, communication, leadership, teamwork, responsible AI, privacy, intellectual property, and the societal impact of computing.

The course is technology-independent. It teaches professional thinking rather than specific tools or programming languages.

FAQ

Is this course language- or tool-specific?

No. It focuses on professional thinking and is independent of any programming language or software stack.

Who is the target audience?

Undergraduate students in CS, SE, Data Science, AI, Cyber Security, and IT.

Does progress save?

Yes — progress is stored in the browser (localStorage) so you can resume across sessions on the same device.
