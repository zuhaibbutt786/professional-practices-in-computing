About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing is designed for undergraduate students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity. It integrates contemporary issues from AI ethics, responsible data science, cybersecurity ethics, open source, entrepreneurship, and digital society.

Unlike traditional courses focused only on software engineering ethics, this course emphasizes professional thinking, ethical reasoning, communication, leadership, and lifelong learning — independent of any specific programming language or tool.

◎

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply professional codes of ethics (ACM, IEEE) to real-world computing dilemmas
  • Analyze privacy, security, and AI ethics issues from multiple stakeholder perspectives
  • Communicate technical and ethical reasoning clearly in writing and presentations
  • Demonstrate teamwork, leadership, and conflict-resolution skills in project contexts
  • Design inclusive, accessible, and sustainable computing solutions
  • Build a professional identity, brand, and continuous learning plan
