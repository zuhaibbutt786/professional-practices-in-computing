About the Course

Professional Practices in Computing prepares future computing professionals to navigate the ethical, legal, social, organisational, and professional challenges of the rapidly evolving technology landscape.

Unlike traditional courses focused only on software engineering ethics, this course integrates contemporary issues from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Open Source, Entrepreneurship, and Digital Society. It is technology-independent and teaches professional thinking rather than tools.
