Lecture 04 — ACM & IEEE Codes Deep Dive

Professional codes as living guidance: structure, key principles, interpretation in real decisions, and limits.

Learning Objectives

  • Locate and explain the main sections of the ACM Code of Ethics and IEEE Code of Ethics.
  • Map specific code clauses to concrete professional situations (privacy, safety, honesty, public interest).
  • Use codes as decision aids while recognizing they do not remove judgment or conflict.
  • Discuss enforcement, organizational culture, and individual responsibility.

Why Codes Matter

Codes articulate shared expectations of the profession. They support education, provide a common language for escalation, and signal accountability to the public and employers. They are not detailed rulebooks for every case.

ACM Code — High-Level Structure

  • General Ethical Principles (contribute to society, avoid harm, be honest, be fair, respect privacy, honor confidentiality).
  • Professional Responsibilities (high quality, maintain competence, respect laws, provide reviews, give realistic evaluations).
  • Professional Leadership Principles (encourage public good, manage personnel and resources responsibly).
  • Compliance with the Code.

IEEE Code — Core Themes

  • Safety, health, and welfare of the public.
  • Honesty and realism in claims and estimates.
  • Rejection of bribery and conflicts of interest that compromise judgment.
  • Fair treatment, continuous improvement, and technical competence.

Multi-Perspective Mapping

Software Engineering

Quality, realistic estimates, and documentation obligations map directly to code clauses.

AI & Data Science

Avoid harm, fairness, and privacy principles guide dataset and model decisions.

Cybersecurity

Responsible disclosure and public welfare align with professional duties.

Leadership

Codes address managers’ duties to create environments that support ethical practice.

Summary
