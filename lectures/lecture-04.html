Lecture 04 — ACM & IEEE Codes Deep Dive

Structure, key principles, and practical application of the primary professional codes for computing practitioners.

Learning Objectives

  • Navigate the structure of the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.
  • Identify corresponding themes in the IEEE Code of Ethics.
  • Map code principles to everyday design, testing, and deployment decisions.
  • Use codes as language for escalating concerns inside organizations.

Motivation

When you need to argue that a release should be delayed, that a dataset should not be used, or that a vulnerability must be disclosed, codes give you shared vocabulary that managers, auditors, and peers recognize.

ACM Code — High-Level Map

  • General Ethical Principles: Contribute to society, avoid harm, be honest, be fair, respect privacy, honor confidentiality.
  • Professional Responsibilities: Strive for high quality, maintain competence, know and respect laws, accept review, give thorough evaluations, honor contracts, improve public understanding, access computing only when authorized.
  • Professional Leadership Principles: Articulate social responsibilities, manage personnel and resources, ensure quality processes, etc.
  • Compliance: Uphold and promote the code; treat violations seriously.

Multi-Perspective Mapping

Software Engineering

“Strive to achieve high quality” and “accept and provide appropriate professional review” directly support peer review, testing standards, and post-incident learning.

AI & Data Science

“Be fair and take action not to discriminate” and “respect privacy” underpin bias audits, consent practices, and model documentation.

Cybersecurity

“Access computing resources only when authorized” and responsible disclosure expectations guide ethical research and incident handling.

Business & Legal

Codes help demonstrate that an organization met (or failed) a recognized professional standard of care.

Application Exercise

A product manager asks the team to disable logging of certain safety-related events to improve performance before a launch. Which ACM principles are engaged? How would you document and escalate the concern?

Summary

ACM and IEEE codes translate high-level ethics into concrete professional obligations. Practitioners use them for self-guidance, team norms, and escalation language.

Cheat Sheet

  • Public good and avoid harm come first
  • Competence + honesty about limits
  • Privacy, fairness, confidentiality
  • Codes = shared language for difficult conversations
