Module 04

ACM & IEEE Codes Deep Dive

How to read professional codes as decision tools—not posters—and apply ACM and IEEE principles to real conflicts in software, data, AI, and security work.

Reading ~40 minCases + Quiz
🎯

Learning Objectives

  • Map ACM Code of Ethics principles to concrete professional decisions.
  • Use IEEE Code of Ethics clauses when evaluating safety, honesty, and public interest.
  • Resolve apparent conflicts between code principles using structured reasoning.
  • Connect code duties to organizational processes (review, escalation, documentation).
📖

Introduction

Codes of ethics are not optional decoration. ACM and IEEE codes articulate duties that members of the profession accept: contribute to society, avoid harm, be honest, respect privacy, honor intellectual property, and take responsibility for work. This module treats codes as operational guidance for design, review, and escalation.

Practice tip

When stuck, write the principle number, the facts, the options, and which stakeholders are affected. Codes become usable when paired with evidence and alternatives.

🧭

ACM Principles (Working View)

1. Public

Contribute to society and human well-being; prioritize public good over narrow employer interest when they conflict seriously.

2. Avoid harm

Take care that systems do not cause unjustified injury—physical, psychological, financial, or rights-based.

3. Honesty

Be truthful about capabilities, limitations, risks, and data quality; do not misrepresent systems.

4. Fairness

Discriminate only on relevant criteria; watch for biased outcomes and exclusionary design.

5. Intellectual property

Respect copyright, patents, trade secrets, and license terms; give credit.

6–8. Privacy, confidentiality, competence

Protect personal data; keep commitments; only claim work within competence or get help.

⚡

IEEE Emphasis

IEEE stresses holding paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public; disclosing factors that might endanger the public; being honest and realistic in claims; and rejecting bribery. For computing professionals building infrastructure, medical software, autonomous systems, or critical data pipelines, these clauses are daily tools.

🏭

Case Application

Scenario: Your team is asked to dark-pattern a subscription cancel flow and to understate model error rates in a sales deck. Which code principles are implicated, and what sequence of actions is professionally defensible?

Map at least three principles, list stakeholders, and propose an internal escalation path before any external step.

Knowledge Check

✍️

Reflection

Pick one ACM or IEEE principle. Describe a workplace decision where applying it would change the design or the release plan. What evidence would you collect?

📋

Summary

  • Codes encode professional duties to the public, clients, and colleagues.
  • Use principles as decision checklists with facts, options, and stakeholders.
  • Document reasoning; escalate when organizational pressure conflicts with core duties.
