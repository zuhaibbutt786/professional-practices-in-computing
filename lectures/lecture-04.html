ACM & IEEE Codes Deep Dive

Structure, key principles, and practical application of the two most influential computing codes of ethics.

Module 04~50–70 minProfessional Standards

Learning Objectives

  • Locate and interpret the main sections of the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.
  • Explain the priority of public safety, health, and welfare in the IEEE Code of Ethics.
  • Map specific code clauses to common workplace scenarios (privacy, safety, honesty, conflicts of interest).
  • Discuss limits of codes as soft law and the role of organizational culture.

Introduction

The ACM and IEEE codes are the primary reference documents for computing professionalism in many universities and organizations. They are living documents that have been revised to address privacy, accessibility, and emerging technologies. This lecture treats them as tools for daily practice, not merely documents to memorize.

ACM Code Highlights

The ACM Code organizes principles around general ethical responsibilities, professional responsibilities, professional leadership principles, and compliance. Key themes include contributing to society, avoiding harm, being honest and trustworthy, respecting privacy, and honoring confidentiality.

IEEE Code Highlights

IEEE places the safety, health, and welfare of the public first. It addresses conflicts of interest, honest disclosure of limitations, and continuous competence. The public-welfare priority is especially relevant for safety-critical and infrastructure systems.

Industrial Scenario

Your manager asks you to understate known limitations of a model in a client proposal. ACM and IEEE both emphasize honesty and accurate communication of capabilities and risks. How would you cite the relevant clauses and propose an alternative path?

Knowledge Check

Reflection

  1. Which ACM or IEEE principle is most often under pressure in product organizations?
  2. How should codes treat generative AI systems that can produce harmful content at scale?

Assignment

Choose three ACM principles and three IEEE principles. For each, write a short workplace vignette (100–150 words) showing the principle under tension and a professional response consistent with the code.

Cheat Sheet

  • ACM: society, avoid harm, honesty, privacy, confidentiality
  • IEEE: public safety/health/welfare first
  • Codes = soft law + cultural tools
  • Cite clauses; propose alternatives

Glossary

Soft law
Norms that guide behavior and can influence legal interpretation without being statutes.
Conflict of interest
Situation in which personal or organizational interests may compromise professional judgment.
