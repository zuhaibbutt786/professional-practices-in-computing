Lecture 01

Introduction to Professional Practices in Computing

Why professionalism matters in computing, building professional identity, and the roadmap for ethical and responsible technology work.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why professional practices are essential in modern computing careers.
  • Distinguish between technical competence and professional responsibility.
  • Identify key stakeholders affected by computing decisions.
  • Recognize the scope of this course across ethics, law, society, and career development.

Motivation

Computing professionals shape systems that influence privacy, safety, fairness, and opportunity at scale. Technical skill alone is insufficient; judgment, accountability, and ethical awareness determine whether technology serves or harms society.

Real Story

Incidents such as large-scale data breaches, biased automated decisions, and safety failures in complex software systems illustrate that professional failures often stem from organizational culture, incomplete risk assessment, and weak accountability—not only from coding errors.
