Lecture 01 — Introduction to Professional Practices

Why professionalism matters in computing

Learning Objectives

  • Define professional practices in the context of computing disciplines
  • Explain why ethical and professional competence is required by ACM and IEEE
  • Identify the multi-perspective nature of modern computing dilemmas
  • Describe the structure and expectations of this course

Introduction

Computing professionals shape systems that affect billions of people. From recommendation algorithms to medical AI and critical infrastructure, the decisions you make as a designer, developer, data scientist, or security engineer carry real consequences. This course prepares you to reason about those consequences systematically.

Motivation & Real Story

When the Therac-25 radiation therapy machine delivered fatal overdoses in the 1980s, the root causes were not only software bugs but also inadequate safety culture, poor communication between engineers and operators, and insufficient independent review. Professional practices exist to reduce the chance that such failures recur.

Industrial Scenario

You are a junior engineer at a startup building a generative AI coding assistant. Your product team wants to ship a feature that automatically includes code snippets from public GitHub repositories. Legal has not yet signed off on license compliance. What do you do?
