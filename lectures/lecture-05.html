Professional Codes & Responsibility

ACM and IEEE codes in depth; whistleblowing, accountability, and organizational ethics.

Learning Objectives

  • Summarize key principles of the ACM and IEEE codes of ethics.
  • Apply code principles to concrete workplace scenarios.
  • Explain when escalation and whistleblowing become professional duties.
  • Distinguish individual responsibility from organizational accountability.

Motivation

Codes of ethics translate abstract ideals into shared expectations for a profession. They are not law, but they guide judgment, hiring, peer review, and public trust. Computing professionals are expected to know and use them.

ACM Code (high-level map)

General ethical principles (contribute to society, avoid harm, be honest, be fair, respect privacy, honor confidentiality), professional responsibilities (high quality, maintain competence, respect laws, accept review, give thorough evaluations), and leadership principles (encourage acceptance of responsibilities, manage personnel and resources ethically, enhance quality of working life).

IEEE Code (high-level map)

Commitment to public safety, health, and welfare; honesty and realism in claims; rejection of bribery; technical competence; fair treatment of others; continuous improvement; and support for colleagues in ethical conduct.

Use codes as tools: When stuck, quote a principle in a design review or incident postmortem. Shared language reduces personalization of conflict.

Whistleblowing & Escalation

Professionals first use internal channels: manager, ethics office, legal, skip-level. Whistleblowing to external parties is a last resort when serious harm is likely and internal avenues fail or are corrupted. Document facts, protect evidence lawfully, and seek advice when possible.

Industrial Scenario

You discover that a safety-critical threshold was lowered to meet a launch date. Your manager says the risk is acceptable. The code of ethics you follow emphasizes public safety. Outline your next three professional steps.

Knowledge Check

Reflection

Reflection

Which single principle from ACM or IEEE feels most relevant to the kind of work you want to do? Why?

Assignment

Select three ACM or IEEE principles. For each, invent a short workplace vignette and state what a professional response would look like (400–600 words total).

Summary

Codes make professional expectations explicit. Use them to guide daily decisions, escalate risk, and build cultures where speaking up is normal rather than heroic.
